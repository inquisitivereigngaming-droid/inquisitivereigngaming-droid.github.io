Twitch VOD Pipeline

Twitch VOD Pipeline is a personal tool used by a single streamer to turn their own stream recordings into highlight clips and upload them to their own YouTube channel on a schedule.

It is not a public service. It does not offer accounts to other users and only ever connects to the owner's YouTube channel.

What it uses Google access for

  • Upload videos (YouTube upload scope): to publish highlight clips and set their thumbnails on the owner's channel.
  • View YouTube account data (YouTube read-only scope): to read view and like counts for the owner's own uploaded videos.
